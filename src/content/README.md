# Content source boundary

Only reviewed, publishable content belongs here. Generated Stitch copy, audit examples, and plausible placeholder text are not approved content.

Before adding a record or document, confirm its owner, source, publication status, and any required legal or factual review. Keep unresolved company details, legal copy, vehicles, prices, commercial claims, dates, articles, images, fonts, and form-processing behavior out of production data until they are verified.

Route slugs and internal links must use the explicit approved route registry and pass the foundation validator. Runtime content fetching and a runtime CMS are outside Phase 1.
