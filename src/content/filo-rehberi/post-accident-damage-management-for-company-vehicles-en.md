## Protect people first

Drivers should move to safety where possible, contact emergency services when required and follow lawful instructions. The fleet process must never encourage evidence collection where doing so would create danger.

## Capture and notify

When safe, record location, time, vehicles, parties, witnesses and relevant photographs. Use the required accident documentation and notify the fleet contact, insurer or leasing provider within the applicable time. Drivers need a simple, accessible checklist in the vehicle or on an approved channel.

## Decide whether the vehicle can move

Visible damage may conceal safety issues. Use professional assistance where drivability is uncertain and arrange recovery through the authorised process. Record the vehicle and destination to maintain custody and communication.

## Coordinate the claim and repair

Create one case record containing evidence, approvals, workshop updates, replacement mobility and cost. Track assessment, parts, repair and quality-control stages separately so delay can be escalated to the responsible party.

## Return safely to operation

Confirm repair completion, required inspection, documents and vehicle condition before reassignment. Update maintenance and damage history and close replacement arrangements promptly.

## Learn without discouraging reports

Review causes, locations, vehicle suitability and process performance. Use patterns to improve training, parking, routes or specification. A fair reporting culture supports early notification and better risk data.
