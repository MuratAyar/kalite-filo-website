## Purpose and ownership

A fleet policy explains how company vehicles are allocated, used and controlled. Name the policy owner, approval authority and review cycle. State which employees, contractors and vehicle classes are covered.

## Eligibility and allocation

Define whether vehicles are role-required, pooled or offered by grade. Record licence checks, probation rules, authorised drivers and the process when an employee changes role or leaves.

## Permitted use

Set rules for business and private journeys, passengers, international travel, towing, smoking, pets and vehicle modifications. Requirements should be clear, proportionate and consistent with current law and employment arrangements.

## Driver responsibilities

Cover daily safety checks, cleanliness, energy or fuel cards, mileage records, parking, keys and documents. Explain how defects, warnings, servicing needs, fines and licence changes must be reported.

## Safety and incidents

Provide an immediate procedure for breakdowns and collisions, including emergencies, evidence, third parties and notification. Prohibit unsafe driving and define training or review processes using fair, documented criteria.

## Maintenance, tyres and damage

Identify who books work, approved providers, authorisation thresholds and replacement-mobility rules. Distinguish fair wear from neglect and explain how costs are investigated.

## Data and privacy

If telematics, location or driver data is processed, define purpose, access, retention and employee information in line with applicable data-protection requirements. Collect only what the stated purpose requires.

## Compliance and review

Explain consequences consistently and provide a route for questions or appeals. Obtain legal, HR, tax, health-and-safety and data-protection review for the organisation's circumstances. Train users at handover and reissue the policy when material changes occur.
