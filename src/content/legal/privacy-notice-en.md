## 2. Whose Personal Data Is Processed?

This notice covers people who use the contact form, request an individual or corporate quotation, act as a contact or authorised representative for a prospective or existing corporate customer, or record a commercial electronic communication preference.

## 3. Personal Data That May Be Processed

### 3.1. Contact Form Data

Name, surname, telephone number, email address, subject, message content, communication preference, consent records and technical submission/security records may be processed.

### 3.2. Corporate Quotation Form Data

Contact identity and contact details, job title, province and district, company website, company type and title, tax office province, tax office and tax number, requested vehicle information, lease term, annual mileage, vehicle count, notes, consent records and technical records may be processed.

### 3.3. Individual Quotation Form Data

Name, surname, Turkish identity number where requested, telephone number, email address, requested vehicle information, lease term, annual mileage, vehicle count, campaign code, notes, consent records and technical records may be processed.

### 3.4. Commercial Electronic Communication Preferences

Channel, scope, date and time of consent or refusal, proof of the transaction and records required for the Message Management System (İYS), where applicable, may be processed.

Do not submit special-category personal data or information that is not required by the relevant form.

## 4. Collection Methods

Data is collected electronically when you complete and submit a form, communicate with our team regarding the request, change a communication preference or when security records are created by the technical infrastructure used to transmit and protect the form.

## 5. Purposes of Processing

### 5.1. Communication and Request Management

We may process data to receive, classify and respond to enquiries; verify contact details where necessary; maintain request history; and manage suggestions, complaints and service communications.

### 5.2. Quotation and Pre-Contract Processes

Data may be processed to understand fleet requirements, prepare and present a quotation, assess product or service suitability, communicate during negotiations and carry out steps requested before a contract is concluded.

### 5.3. Operations, Security and Compliance

Processing may support information security, misuse and fraud prevention, audit trails, dispute management, the establishment or protection of rights and compliance with binding legal obligations and requests from authorised bodies.

### 5.4. Commercial Electronic Communications

Where valid consent or another applicable legal basis exists, contact and preference data may be used to send promotional or marketing communications and to manage consent, refusal and İYS records.

## 6. Legal Grounds

Depending on the processing activity, data may be processed because it is directly related to establishing or performing a contract; necessary for the Company's legitimate interests without prejudicing fundamental rights; necessary for establishing, exercising or protecting a right; required to comply with a legal obligation or expressly provided by law; or based on explicit consent where the law requires it.
