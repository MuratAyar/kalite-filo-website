# Cookie Policy

**Last updated:** 27 August 2026

This policy explains the cookies and similar browser storage technologies used on the Kalite Filo website at **https://kalitefilo.com.tr/**. It describes only technologies verified as present on the current website and does not suggest that unused analytics or advertising tools are in operation.

For general information about the processing of personal data, please review the [Data Protection and Security](/en/data-protection-and-security/) page.

## What are cookies and similar technologies?

Cookies and comparable technologies, including local storage, can store information on a device so that a website can function or remember choices expressly made by a user in the same browser. The Kalite Filo website currently uses the local storage records listed below.

## Current technology inventory

| Record | Provider | Purpose | Category | Retention approach |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `kalite-filo-privacy-preferences-v1` | Kalite Filo | Remember the privacy choice and avoid reopening the preference panel on every visit | Strictly necessary | Up to 180 days from the preference date; may be removed sooner if browser data is cleared |
| `kalite-filo-vehicle-cart-v1` | Kalite Filo | Remember vehicles expressly added to the cart, together with quantity, lease term and mileage preferences, in the same browser | Strictly necessary / user-requested functionality | Until the user clears the cart or removes browser data |

These records remain in your browser. The current website code does not contain visitor analytics, behavioural advertising, retargeting or social-media tracking pixels.

## Google Maps content

The map on the Contact page is third-party content supplied by Google Maps. It is not loaded, and no map request is sent to Google, unless you expressly enable **Functional Technologies**.

If enabled, Google may process device and connection information under its own terms while serving the map, and may place cookies or similar records on your device. Google’s current processing and retention practices are not determined by Kalite Filo. You should therefore review Google’s privacy and cookie information before enabling the map.

Declining Google Maps functionality does not prevent use of any other part of the website. The address and contact details remain available instead of the map.

## Preference categories

### Strictly necessary technologies

These technologies are required to provide website functions you request and to remember your privacy selection. They cannot be disabled through the preference panel. The vehicle-cart record is created only when you add a vehicle to the cart.

### Functional technologies

These allow the Google Maps content to load. They are disabled by default and become active only after your express selection.

### Performance, analytics, advertising and marketing

The current website does not use tracking technologies in these categories. If such technology is introduced later, the inventory, preference interface and this policy must be updated before use. Where required, the technology must operate only after a valid prior choice has been obtained.

## How can you manage your preferences?

The **Privacy Preferences** panel displayed on your first visit allows you to:

- reject all optional technologies,
- manage functional technologies separately, or
- approve the optional technologies currently available.

You can change your selection later using the **Privacy Preferences** button in the footer. The preference record expires after 180 days, after which a new choice is requested. Clearing site data in your browser also removes the record.

You may delete all site data or block storage through your browser settings. Doing so may prevent explicitly requested functions, such as the vehicle cart, from being remembered.

## Legal approach

Strictly necessary records are used to provide user-requested website functions and to retain an auditable record of the privacy selection. Optional Google Maps content is loaded only at the user’s choice. Declining it does not restrict basic access to the website or the quotation and contact pages.

Permission for one technology does not constitute general consent to process personal data for unrelated purposes. Transparency and consent processes are assessed separately.

## Contact and requests

Questions about this policy or your privacy preferences may be sent to **info@kalitefilo.com.tr**. Procedures for personal-data requests and verified controller information should be provided on the [Data Protection and Security](/en/data-protection-and-security/) page.

## Changes to this policy

If a new third-party service, analytics tool or advertising technology is introduced, this inventory, the preference interface and the relevant privacy notices must be updated together before the technology is used. The current revision date is shown at the beginning of this page.
