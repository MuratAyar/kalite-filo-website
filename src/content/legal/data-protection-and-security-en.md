## 2. Scope

This text provides a general framework for customers, prospective customers, corporate representatives and contacts, website users, suppliers, business partners and other people who interact with the Company. A process-specific privacy notice takes precedence where it provides more detailed information.

## 3. Core Principles

Personal data is processed lawfully and fairly, kept accurate where necessary, used for specified and legitimate purposes, limited to what is relevant, and retained only for the period required by the purpose and applicable legislation.

## 4. Categories of Personal Data

Depending on the relationship and service, the Company may process identity; contact; customer and transaction; financial; legal and compliance; system-security and technical; marketing preference; enquiry, complaint and communication; and cookie or similar-technology data.

Special-category data is not requested through general website forms and should not be submitted unless specifically required through an approved process and protected by an applicable legal condition.

## 5. Collection Channels

Data may be obtained directly from the individual, an authorised corporate representative, contracts and forms, telephone or email communications, the corporate Website, service processes, legally authorised public sources, suppliers or business partners, and security or transaction records created by information systems.

## 6. Processing Purposes

Data may be used to manage enquiries and quotations; conduct pre-contract and contractual processes; provide fleet-related services; manage customers, suppliers and business partners; invoice and collect; coordinate maintenance, assistance, claims and damage; meet legal obligations; establish or protect rights; prevent misuse; secure systems; maintain audit records; improve operations; and send commercial communications where a valid legal basis exists.

## 7. Legal Grounds

Processing relies on one or more conditions permitted by the KVKK, including express provision by law, necessity for a contract, compliance with a legal obligation, establishment or protection of a right, legitimate interests that do not prejudice fundamental rights, information made public by the person where legally applicable, and explicit consent where required.

## 8. Main Processing Activities

| Activity | Typical data | Main purpose |

| Contact and quotation | Identity, contact, company and request details | Responding and pre-contract work |

## 12. Information Security

### 12.1. Technical Measures

Depending on risk, controls may include access restriction, authentication, logging, secure configuration, backups, malware protection, encryption in appropriate contexts, vulnerability and patch management, network controls and monitoring.

### 12.2. Administrative Measures

Measures may include role and authority management, confidentiality commitments, staff awareness, supplier review, data minimisation, incident procedures, continuity planning and periodic control review.

### 12.3. Responsible Security Statement

No electronic environment can be guaranteed absolutely secure. The Company applies measures proportionate to known risks and applicable obligations and reviews them as systems and threats change.
